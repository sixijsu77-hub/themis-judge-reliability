1. The null distribution of the slot-skew S, and the fixed threshold 0.05
==============================================================================
  50th percentile of S under uniform letters : 0.0400
  95th percentile of S under uniform letters : 0.0750
  99th percentile of S under uniform letters : 0.0900

  0.05 sits at the 69.1th percentile of that distribution.
  An unbiased judge exceeds it 30.9% of the time.
  A threshold inside the noise cannot separate no bias from some bias.
