are the four arrangements of one item independent?

Intervals bootstrapped over items, and the simulated nulls behind the decision
rules, draw an item's four arrangements independently. This measures whether they
are, on the indicator the position hypotheses read: did the judge answer A.

  obvious      ICC  design effect  verdicts  effective n
        3   0.0000          1.000       600          600
        2   0.0000          1.000       600          600
        1   0.0000          1.000       600          600
        0   0.1227          1.368       600          439

  Zero at the three levels where the judge is mostly right, and positive on the
  unmodified item, where the same item tends to draw the same letter whatever the
  arrangement. Where it is positive, intervals are widened by the design effect
  and that is stated with the result.
